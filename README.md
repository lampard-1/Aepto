## 📖 Overview

**[Aepto](https://aepto.com/)** is an **AI domain monitoring and hosting management platform** that brings all your online assets (domains, websites and hosting) into one intelligent, fast dashboard. It simplifies how you manage, protect and monitor your digital presence, **no matter where your domains or hosting are**.

Add a domain with a single click, with no integrations needed, and Aepto scans your DNS, email setup and hosting, scores its health and alerts you before small problems become costly outages.

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Intelligence

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🧠 **Smart Domain Insights** | AI scans DNS records, website and email hosting locations, SPF, DKIM and other essential email settings, then produces a **health score** with actionable suggestions. |
| 🔎 **AI Domain Discovery** | Instantly generate available domain names from your keywords, brand or concept. |
| 🌐 **AI Website & Content Monitoring** | Detects downtime, slow loading and real issues like plugin failures, database crashes or hacks, and alerts you early. |

### 🛡️ Protection & Monitoring

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🔐 **Domain Protection & Theft Guard** | 24/7 monitoring of DNS, name servers and lock status, with instant alerts on suspicious changes. |
| 🌍 **Global Uptime Monitoring** | Track uptime from multiple regions across Asia, Europe and the USA, so you know exactly where downtime occurs. |
| 📶 **Website & DNS Monitoring** | Continuous checks on your sites and DNS with configurable check intervals. |
| 🔒 **SSL Certificate Alerts** | Get warned before certificates expire. |
| 📧 **Email Deliverability Scanning** | Catch SPF, DKIM and email configuration problems before they affect delivery. |
| 📝 **Content Monitoring** | Detect unexpected changes to your website content. |

### 📊 Management & Finance

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 💸 **Domain Expense & ROI Tracking** | Track purchase costs, renewals, estimated value and ROI across your portfolio. |
| 📉 **Cost Analysis & Budget Management** | Detailed expense breakdowns to plan budgets and control domain and hosting spend. |
| ⏰ **Flexible Renewal Reminders** | Set reminders a month, a week or on a specific date ahead, so you never lose a domain. |
| 👀 **Domain Watch** | Track domains you want to buy and get notified when they're about to expire or become available. |
| 🗂️ **Domain Folder Management** | Organize domains into folders, from a handful to hundreds. |
| 🏦 **Works with Every Registrar** | Manage everything in one place. Aepto auto-detects hosting setups and helps you fix issues quickly. |

### 📣 Status Pages

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 📡 **Public Status Pages** | Professional, real-time status pages showing uptime, performance and errors. |
| 🏷️ **White-Label & Custom Domains** | Brand status pages with your own domain for teams, clients or agencies (on higher plans). |

### ⚡ Experience

- **Ultra-fast, easy-to-use interface**, whether you manage a handful of domains or hundreds
- **One-click domain addition**, with no integrations required
- **Instant navigation** between domains, websites and hosting
- **Smart alerts** and **centralized insights** in a single dashboard
